## 1. Figure Production Standards

To maintain total scientific reproducibility across papers, presentations, and documentation:
1. **Scripted Generation:** No figure may be created or retouched manually in vector editing tools. Every chart, plot, and diagram must be programmatically generated via scripts committed to `mocs/benchmark/plots/`.
2. **Standard Aesthetics:** Consistent styling using matplotlib/seaborn (300+ DPI, CMYK/RGB dual-export, colorblind-safe palettes, standard LaTeX font embedding).
3. **Provenance Grounding:** Every data point in empirical plots must map directly to an authenticated CSV record in `results/` generated by MOBench.

### Figure 2: AABB Distance Bounding and Periodic Patch Decomposition
* **Target Location:** Section 4 of Primary Paper.
* **Title:** Conservative Spatial Bounding and Periodic Boundary Face Unwrapping.
* **Caption:** *(A) Spatial bounding of atomic coordinates over temporal block $B_m$ using an Axis-Aligned Bounding Box (AABB) $\mathcal{B}_i^m$. (B) Derivation of minimum separation $L$ and maximum separation $U$ between selection boxes via Minkowski interval projections. (C) Periodic patch decomposition (guarded conservative PBC bounding algorithm): when an atom crosses cell face $x = 0 \leftrightarrow x = L_x$, coordinates are partitioned into spatial clusters and unwrapped by $+L_x$, with multi-crossing blocks falling back to exact frame evaluation.*
* **Visual Layout:**

### Figure 4: Hierarchical Dyadic Refinement and Monotonic Bound Non-Expansion
* **Target Location:** Section 6 of Primary Paper.
* **Title:** Hierarchical Temporal Block Refinement over Ambiguous Intervals.
* **Caption:** *Selective refinement of an ambiguous (`UNKNOWN`) temporal block. (A) Level 0 coarse block (500 frames) straddles threshold $\theta = 4.0\ \text{Å}$ ($L=3.1\ \text{Å}, U=5.2\ \text{Å}$). (B) Dyadic subdivision into sub-blocks yields monotonic non-increasing bounding interval widths ($W_{\text{child}} \le W_{\text{parent}}$). Left sub-block [0, 250) tightens to $U=3.8\ \text{Å} < \theta$, terminating as `CERTIFIED_TRUE`. (C) Right sub-block [250, 500) remains ambiguous and subdivides further until exact coordinate reading resolves the leaf.*
